Delete your account

Where R My People (Family Compass)

You can permanently delete your Where R My People account and the data the app keeps about you at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Where R My People and sign in.
  2. On the compass screen, tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account…
  4. Enter your password to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

You will be returned to the sign-in screen. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer open the app (for example, you uninstalled it or lost access to your phone), email ai.bri.9087@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my Where R My People account.” We will verify the request and delete your account, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password)
  • Your display name
  • Your shared location, if any is currently stored
  • All of your family connections (you also disappear from the other person’s family list)
  • Invite codes you created

What may remain

  • If you accepted an invite created by someone else, that invite record keeps an anonymous ID number (no name, email, or location) until its creator deletes it or deletes their own account.
  • Google may retain deleted data in backups or system logs for a limited time under its own retention practices.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or data — use one of the options above first. To stop sharing without deleting anything, just turn off Share with family in the app.
